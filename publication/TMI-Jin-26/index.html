D²Mamba: Dual Dynamic State Space Model for Curvilinear Structure Segmentation

Yufei Jin Dalian University of Technology (DUT)
Han Yang Dalian University of Technology (DUT)
Aojun Jiang University of Toronto

PDF Cite

Abstract

Curvilinear structure segmentation plays a vital role in biomedical image analysis, particularly in tasks such as vessel segmentation, nerve fiber detection, and sperm tail tracking. Accurate segmentation of these thin, elongated structures remains challenging due to their low contrast, complex morphologies, and noisy backgrounds in microscopy images.

In this paper, we propose D²Mamba, a dual dynamic state space model designed for curvilinear structure segmentation. Our approach leverages the selective scanning mechanism of Mamba to capture long-range dependencies along curvilinear structures while maintaining computational efficiency. The dual-branch architecture enables simultaneous modeling of local细节 and global context, leading to improved segmentation accuracy.

Extensive experiments on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that D²Mamba achieves state-of-the-art performance in curvilinear structure segmentation tasks, outperforming existing methods in terms of both accuracy and efficiency. The proposed method shows strong generalization ability across different imaging modalities and biological structures.
